# Code of conduct

Helm follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and
accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the community. Harassment, insults and
personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/sovanken/helm-term/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove
content or block participants who break these rules.
